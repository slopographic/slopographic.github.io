FONTLOG for the Slopographic font family

Basic Font Information
----------------------

Slopographic is the type family of slopographic.com: a sans, a serif, a mono and a condensed face made from open-source (SIL OFL) fonts, with drop-shaped dots on i and j, a single-storey a and a flat-top 3 in the sans, a dripping "slop" ligature, and the Slopographic drop mark in the Private Use Area. The mascot is not in the fonts: Sloppo stays all-rights-reserved art.

Version 1.000. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).

Styles and their sources
------------------------

- Slopographic Sans: modified from Inter Tight (Copyright 2022 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter-tight)).
    Axes: wght 300-900.
    Upstream alternates made the default: cv11, cv09.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: drop.
    ss01: Double-storey a.
    ss02: Round-top 3.
    ss03: Round dots.
    ss04: Open digits.
    dlig: Drip ligature (slop, Slop).
    Private Use Area marks: mark.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
- Slopographic Serif: modified from Instrument Serif (Copyright 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors (https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif)).
    The dots of i and j redrawn: drop.
    dlig: Drip ligature (slop, Slop).
    Private Use Area marks: mark.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
- Slopographic Mono: modified from JetBrains Mono (Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono)).
    Axes: wght 400-800.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: drop.
    ss01: Round dots.
    dlig: Drip ligature (slop, Slop).
    Private Use Area marks: mark.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
- Slopographic Condensed: modified from Archivo (Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo)).
    Axes: wdth 62-100, wght 300-900.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: drop.
    ss01: Round dots.
    dlig: Drip ligature (slop, Slop).
    Private Use Area marks: mark.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
