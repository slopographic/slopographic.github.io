# Glossary: Chinese, Traditional (zh-TW)

Register: address the user as 您, as Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace in Taiwan do. Use Taiwan vocabulary, not converted Simplified: 檔案 (not 文件) for file, 資料夾, 設定, 應用程式, 列印, 儲存, 網路, 程式碼, 字型, 分頁. Buttons are bare verbs (開啟, 儲存, 匯出). Full-width punctuation with 「」 quotes; a half-width space between Chinese and Latin words or numbers. Brand and app names stay in Latin script.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/zh-TW.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
