# Glossary: Hebrew (he)

Register: gender-neutral, as Google Workspace and current Microsoft 365 Hebrew do. Buttons and commands are action nouns («שמירה», «פתיחה», «ביטול», «סגירה»), never masculine imperatives; running text avoids gendered verbs by using impersonal forms («אפשר לשנות», «בוחרים כאן») and possessives («הקבצים שלך»). Hebrew is right to left; Latin brand names, keys and numbers are mixed in as is, and a Hebrew prefix before a Latin word takes a hyphen (ב-Slop Drive, ו-Edge). Keep labels short.
