# Glossary: Spanish (es)

Register: address the user as tú, as shared/i18n/es.js does and as Google Workspace in Spanish does (Elígelo, tus archivos); buttons are infinitives (Abrir, Guardar, Exportar), messages use the imperative (Suelta, Guarda). Peninsular-neutral vocabulary: archivo, carpeta, ordenador. Brand and app names stay as they are.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/es.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
