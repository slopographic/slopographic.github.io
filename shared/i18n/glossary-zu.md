# Glossary: Zulu (zu)

Register: Address the user with singular "wena" (possessives "-kho": "amafayela akho"), as Microsoft and Google isiZulu UIs do. Commands and buttons are bare imperatives ("Vula", "Londoloza", "Khansela"). Loanwords and brand names take a hyphenated class prefix (i-app, ama-app, i-Chrome, ku-Slop Drive); keep the brand itself unchanged after the hyphen. Quality is beta: awaiting native review.
