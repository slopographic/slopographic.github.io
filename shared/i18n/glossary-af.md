# Glossary: Afrikaans (af)

Register: informal «jy/jou», as Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 do in Afrikaans. Buttons and commands are imperatives, with separable verbs split as Microsoft writes them («Maak oop», «Maak toe», «Voer uit»). Negatives take the double «nie» («Geen rekening nodig nie»). The indefinite article is written ’n. Quotes are “dubbel”. Keep labels short.
