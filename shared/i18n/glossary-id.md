# Glossary: Indonesian (id)

Register: address the user as Anda (capitalised), as Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace in Indonesian do; buttons are bare verbs (Buka, Simpan, Ekspor). Common loanwords the big products keep stay as they are (file, folder, browser, offline, default). Brand and app names stay as they are.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/id.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
