# Glossary: French (fr)

Register: address the user as vous, as shared/i18n/fr.js does and as Microsoft Office, Google Workspace and Adobe Acrobat in French do; buttons are infinitives (Ouvrir, Enregistrer, Exporter), messages use the vous imperative (Choisissez-le). Typographic apostrophe (’), guillemets « » with spaces, a space before : ? ! and %. Brand and app names stay as they are.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/fr.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
