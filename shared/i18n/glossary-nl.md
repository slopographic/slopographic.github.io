# Glossary: Dutch (nl)

Register: informal «je/jij», as current Microsoft Office, Google Workspace and Adobe Acrobat do in Dutch (Microsoft moved from «u» to «je»). Buttons and commands are infinitives («Opslaan», «Openen», «Annuleren»), as in Office; menu titles are nouns («Bestand», «Bewerken», «Beeld»). Plural of PDF is «pdf’s». Quotes are ‘enkel’, as in Microsoft’s Dutch style guide. Keep labels short.
