# Glossary: Chinese, Simplified (zh-CN)

Register: address the user as 你 (not 您), as current Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace Simplified Chinese do. Commands and buttons are bare verbs (打开, 保存, 导出), no 请 on buttons; 请 is fine in a full sentence asking the user to act. Use full-width punctuation (，。：？（）“”) in Chinese text and keep a half-width space between Chinese and Latin words or numbers (在 Slop Drive 中, 约 60%). Brand and app names stay in Latin script.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/zh-CN.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
