# Glossary: Korean (ko)

Register: formal polite 합니다/습니다 style in sentences and messages and the -세요 form for requests, as Microsoft Office and Google Workspace in Korean do; buttons and menu items are nouns or noun phrases (저장, 열기, 내보내기, 앱 열기). Particles after Latin words or placeholders use the double form 와(과), 을(를) when the word is unknown. Brand and app names stay in Latin script.

The terms the shared chrome (shared/i18n/ko.js) and the app tables use. Add a term here when you choose one.
