# Glossary: German (de)

Register: formal, without direct address where possible. Buttons and commands are infinitives («Speichern», «Öffnen», «Abbrechen»); menu titles are nouns («Datei», «Bearbeiten», «Ansicht», «Hilfe»). Tooltips, hints and messages are impersonal («Ziehen ändert die Größe», «Die Änderungen gehen verloren»), as Office tooltips are; shared/i18n/ui/de.js follows this. When address cannot be avoided, use «Sie», as Microsoft and Google do. shared/i18n/de.js uses «Sie» throughout (no «du»). Quotes are „deutsch“. German runs long: keep buttons short; hyphenate compounds after brand names («Slopographic-Apps»).
