# Glossary: Hindi (hi)

Register: address the user as आप with polite imperatives (खोलें, सहेजें, रद्द करें), as Microsoft Office and Google Workspace in Hindi do. Prefer the everyday tech words those products use (फ़ाइल, ऐप, टैब, सेटिंग) over Sanskritised coinages; keep nukta spellings (फ़ाइल, फ़ोल्डर, ऑफ़लाइन). Sentences end with the danda (।). Brand and app names and units (GB, TB) stay in Latin script.
