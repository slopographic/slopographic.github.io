# Slop Watermark test fixtures

- `two-frames.gif` and `two-frames.webp`: 80 × 60 animations of two flat frames (blue, then green), 300 ms each, made for
  these tests with Pillow. Made by Slopographic and dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0); no third-party material.
- The video and its sound come from `tests/convert/fixtures/tone.mp4` (a 2-second, 160 × 120 clip with a tone).
