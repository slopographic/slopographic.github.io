# Slopographic brand policy

**Slopographic™**, **Sloppo™**, their logos and the Sloppo character are trademarks of Slopographic. The code is MIT (see `LICENSE`), but the MIT License gives no rights to the brand.

## Fine without asking
- Talking about Slopographic or Sloppo, reviewing it, linking to it, writing tutorials.
- Saying your project "works with Slopographic" or "is a fork of Slopographic", as long as it's clear it isn't the official one.
- Fan art of Sloppo for personal, non-commercial use.

## Needs written permission
- Using "Slopographic" or "Sloppo" in the name of your app, fork, website, domain, company or product.
- Using the logo or the Sloppo character in your own project, or on merchandise.
- Anything that could make people think you are Slopographic or endorsed by it.

## Forks
Forks are welcome under MIT. Before you publish one, rename it, replace the logo and remove or replace Sloppo (the files listed in `LICENSE-ART`).

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on the repository.
