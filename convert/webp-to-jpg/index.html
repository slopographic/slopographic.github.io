WebP to JPG converter

Turn WebP images into JPG for apps and sites that do not accept WebP. Transparent areas are filled with a colour you choose.

How it works

The WebP is drawn onto a canvas over your chosen background colour, then encoded as JPG.

Quality and size options work as they do for PNG to JPG.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Why choose a background colour?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent pixels need a colour to become.

Can I make the JPG smaller?

Lower the quality or resize the image. Both reduce the file size.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
