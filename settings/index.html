Privacy

Slopographic runs in your browser and your files never leave your device. Your choices on this page are stored in this browser's local storage and nowhere else. Analytics and ads are off until you turn them on, and no third-party script loads until you do. You can change this at any time.

Local folder

Storage

How much room this site uses in your browser, and what uses it. Your files, documents and saved projects are stored here too. Clearing a cache removes only what is made again when needed.

Updates

Slopographic checks for a new version each time you open a page. If a page still looks old, load the latest version: the offline copies are fetched again and the page reloads. Your files, documents, settings and downloaded packs are kept. On any page, adding ?fresh to the address does the same.
